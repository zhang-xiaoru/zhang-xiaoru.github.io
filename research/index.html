Previous graduate research

Nanoscale Surface Acoustic Wave Devices

Graduate research · The University of Texas at Austin

In my first two years of graduate research, I worked on surface acoustic wave (SAW) devices in micro-electro-mechanical systems (MEMS), covering the full device-engineering cycle from simulation and CAD design to nanofabrication and characterization. Beyond electrical measurements with a vector network analyzer, I used transmission-mode microwave impedance microscopy (TMIM) to image electro-mechanical waves inside working devices with sub-micron resolution.

Structured Light & Optical Experiments

Undergraduate research

I developed a Fourier-domain inverse method to recover vector optical modes, implemented it in MATLAB, and programmed a spatial light modulator. I designed and assembled an optical-table setup for experimental verification, connecting computational analysis with hands-on optical measurements.
