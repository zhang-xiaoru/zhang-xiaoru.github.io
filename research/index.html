Computational Materials & Devices

Graduate research · The University of Texas at Austin

I use first-principles calculations to study functional oxides and semiconductor interfaces for electronic and electro-optic integration. My research connects atomic-scale structure and electronic properties with device behavior, including numerical models of silicon-integrated electro-optic reservoirs.

Previous graduate research

Experimental Acoustics & MEMS

Graduate research · The University of Texas at Austin

I investigated how nanoscale devices guide and confine acoustic waves, combining simulation and mask design with fabrication and characterization. Spatial measurements of acoustic propagation, together with SEM and AFM, helped me identify fabrication-related failures and refine designs and processes.

    Topological acoustic devices

    Investigating gigahertz acoustic propagation in patterned phononic structures using spatially resolved measurements.

Undergraduate research

Structured Light & Optical Experiments

Undergraduate research

I developed a Fourier-domain inverse method to recover vector optical modes, implemented it in MATLAB, and programmed a spatial light modulator. I designed and assembled an optical-table setup for experimental verification, connecting computational analysis with hands-on optical measurements.

  • Optical experiments · MATLAB

    Vector optical-mode demultiplexing

    Recovering vector optical modes through Fourier-domain analysis and experimental verification with a spatial light modulator.
