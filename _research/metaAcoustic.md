## Project overview

This study investigates acoustic propagation in nanoscale phononic structures. My qualification presentation brings together device geometry, simulations, and transmission-mode microwave impedance microscopy (TMIM) maps to examine propagation near boundaries between distinct structures.

My experimental work included mapping acoustic propagation and processing microscopy scans to analyze device behavior and identify fabrication-related failures. The qualification presentation records the project’s progress as of August 2024.
