## Project overview

I use first-principles calculations to investigate functional oxides for semiconductor integration. This work includes evaluating band alignment at dielectric interfaces with ultrawide-bandgap GeO₂ and studying BaTiO₃ growth pathways on sapphire.

The broader question is how interface structure and chemistry influence the suitability of a material for electronic or electro-optic integration.
