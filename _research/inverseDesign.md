## Project overview

I developed a MATLAB–COMSOL inverse-design workflow for acoustic power splitters and Python tools to translate design geometries into multilayer CAD masks. The workflow connects simulated wave propagation with fabrication layout requirements.

The qualification presentation compares simulated output powers for different scattering geometries. Its 9:1 splitting ratio is a design target, not a measured experimental result. Fabrication and experimental validation of the splitter were listed as future work in August 2024.
