## Project overview

I develop Python simulations of an electro-optic reservoir based on silicon-integrated BaTiO₃ devices. The numerical study examines prediction and classification performance under practical device constraints.

Parameter studies and benchmark comparisons connect the behavior of the physical model with its computing performance.
