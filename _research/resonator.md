## Project overview

This work examines acoustic resonators through spatial measurements of confinement and leakage. The qualification presentation includes noise-floor measurements and comparisons of signals inside and outside a resonator, alongside a separate device-design and fabrication study.

These results connect experimental characterization with questions about device geometry and acoustic behavior. Additional electrical testing and remeasurement were listed as future work in August 2024.
