## Project overview

I developed a Fourier-domain inverse method for demultiplexing vector optical modes, implemented the method in MATLAB, and programmed a spatial light modulator. I designed and assembled an optical-table setup for experimental verification.

This work connected computational analysis with optical alignment and measurement. The research received an Outstanding Thesis Award in 2022.
